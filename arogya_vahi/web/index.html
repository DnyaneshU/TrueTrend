    How do I save a report from WhatsApp?
    1. Open the report PDF in WhatsApp.
    2. Tap Share (the arrow at the bottom).
    3. Choose Save to Files, then tap Save.
    4. Come back here and tap Choose a file.
    5. Tap Browse and pick the same file.
